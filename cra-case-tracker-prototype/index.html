    What this prototype demonstrates

    Built as a portfolio concept for taxpayer services roles. It mirrors the workflow of a contact-centre / taxpayer services queue, using fictional cases only.

    • 1
      Case triage
      Sorting new inquiries by category, channel and priority so urgent and time-sensitive cases surface first.
    • 2
      Ticketing & documentation
      Every case carries an ID, dates, owner and a plain-language note of what was asked and what was advised.
    • 3
      Status tracking
      New → In Progress → Waiting on taxpayer → Resolved, so no inquiry sits untracked.
    • 4
      SLA awareness
      Due dates and At risk flags are visible in the queue, not buried — the watchlist is worked first.
    • 5
      Escalation
      Cases needing senior review (for example hardship or business verification) are marked Escalated with the reason in the notes.
